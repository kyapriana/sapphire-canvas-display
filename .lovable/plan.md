

# Kiky's Portfolio Website

A stunning, deep blue and off-white portfolio website showcasing your 10+ years of marketing expertise with engaging animations and interactive elements.

## Design Theme
- **Deep navy blue** backgrounds with **off-white/cream** text and accents
- Clean, modern typography with bold headings
- Smooth scroll animations and hover effects throughout
- Professional yet creative feel matching your headshot aesthetic

## Sections (Single Scrolling Page)

### 1. Hero Section
- Full-screen hero with your professional headshot
- Your name, title ("Marketing Manager at Grab"), and a brief tagline
- Animated gradient background with subtle particle/floating shape effects
- Scroll-down indicator

### 2. About Me
- Your professional summary highlighting 10 years of experience
- Key stats with animated counters: **10+ years experience**, **5+ years in management**, **77% sales increase** achieved
- Smooth fade-in animations as you scroll

### 3. Career Timeline
- Interactive vertical timeline of your career journey
- From Nutrifood (2014) → Transretail → SC Johnson → Wings Group → Frisian Flag → Grab (Present)
- Each role expands on click to show key achievements
- Company names and roles with animated entrance effects

### 4. Awards & Achievements
- Highlight cards for your impressive awards: Grab Way Award, MMA Smarties Gold, Drum Award Bronze
- International debate achievements displayed in a visually engaging grid
- Hover animations on each card

### 5. Skills
- Visual skill display: Networking, Marketing Strategy, Distribution Development, Negotiation, Project Management
- Animated skill bars or interactive tags

### 6. Education
- Jenderal Soedirman University — Bachelor of Economics
- Clean, minimal presentation

### 7. Contact & Footer
- Display your email and location (Jakarta, Indonesia)
- Links to LinkedIn/social profiles (placeholders you can update)
- Option to download your resume as PDF
- Simple, elegant footer

## Interactive Elements
- Scroll-triggered animations on every section
- Animated number counters for key metrics
- Expandable career timeline entries
- Hover effects on cards and buttons
- Smooth page transitions and parallax effects
